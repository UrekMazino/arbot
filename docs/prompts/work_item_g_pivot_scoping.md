## 3. G1 pre-test — locked spec

- **Population:** discovery-gated, same convention (120-bar EG p ≤ 0.05, folds every 20 bars) — the thesis is "*discovered* relationships break with momentum," matching the measured population.
- **Entry:** |z| crosses ≥ z_entry at day-t close → enter at t+1 close, **WITH the extension** (z > 0 → long spread). β-aware $200 gross, one position per pair.
- **Exits (continuation-appropriate, first-of, executed at next close):** trailing stop — exit when z retraces ≥ `trail` σ from its peak favorable extension; reversion stop — z crosses back through (z_entry − 0.5) toward the mean (thesis failed); max hold 20d. No coint-watch exit — breakdown is the thesis, not the risk.
- **Grid (LOCKED, 6 cells):** z_entry ∈ {1.5, 2.0, 2.5} × trail ∈ {0.75, 1.25}σ. Ridge ≥ 2 adjacent qualifying cells (z_entry neighbors at same trail, or the two trails at same z_entry). The grid does not grow.
- **Costs:** Phase A measured — BASE $0.14 + $0.104·(hold/10); STRESS $0.28 + $0.470·(hold/10). Qualifying cells must be net-positive at BOTH.
- **Guards:** n ≥ 30 trades/cell, ≥ 10 distinct pairs, no pair > 40% of cell net, PLUS the §2 per-half sign-stability requirement.
- **No-lookahead:** identical protocol to Phase B (signals from ≤ t, fills at t+1 close).
