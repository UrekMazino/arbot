## Verdict: INSUFFICIENT-OVERLAP-TO-ISOLATE-MECHANISM

The decisive cut — pairs that appear under BOTH RANGE and RISK_OFF regimes with sufficient samples (n≥5 each) — yields only **2 pairs** out of 129 distinct pairs observed across the 18 runs. N=2 paired-pair observations cannot isolate whether the aggregate RISK_OFF elevation (B1 v1: 23.1% broken_rate vs RANGE 16.5%) is **regime-causal** (same pairs become more fragile under RISK_OFF) or **pair-selection-driven** (RISK_OFF just selects different, worse pairs).

A directional read is available from the two paired pairs:

| Pair | rate_RANGE | rate_RISK_OFF | Δ (ROF−RNG) | n_RANGE | n_RISK_OFF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ADA-USDT-SWAP/LINK-USDT-SWAP | 0.0000 | 0.0000 | +0.0000 | 8 | 6 |
| BTC-USDT-SWAP/AVAX-USDT-SWAP | 0.0000 | 0.2857 | **+0.2857** | 6 | 7 |

- ADA/LINK: identical broken_rate under both regimes (0%). Pair-selection-consistent.
- BTC/AVAX: striking elevation under RISK_OFF (0% RANGE → 28.6% RISK_OFF). Regime-causal-consistent.

Sample-weighted mean Δ across the two: **+0.14**. Direction consistent with regime-causal but driven entirely by one pair (BTC/AVAX). N=2 is too thin to generalize.

**The honest read: directional consistent with regime-causal but not isolated.** The aggregate B1 v1 finding (RISK_OFF 23.1% vs RANGE 16.5%) stands; the mechanism (regime-causal vs pair-selection) cannot be cleanly decomposed from this data structure.

## The structural finding that emerged from the cut itself

**Only 2 pairs out of 129 appear under both regimes with sufficient samples.** This is itself informative: the bot's pair-selection covaries strongly with regime. Under each regime the bot tends to select different pair sets; rarely does the same pair appear under both regimes long enough to accumulate meaningful sample counts on each side. The pair-selection mechanism is regime-conditioned at the input layer — before any cointegration test fires.

Consequence: the aggregate RISK_OFF-vs-RANGE comparison from B1 v1 (23.1% vs 16.5%) is **fundamentally entangled** with the bot's regime-conditioned pair selection. The two aren't separable at the experiment-window data:

- **Regime-causal interpretation:** "RISK_OFF causes more breakage on the same pairs"  
- **Pair-selection interpretation:** "RISK_OFF selects pairs that have higher base-rate breakage"  
- **Combined interpretation:** "Both — RISK_OFF selects different pairs AND those pairs may be more fragile under RISK_OFF; the bot's pair-selection logic and the regime both contribute"

A clean discrimination would require either: (a) running the bot with regime-neutral pair selection (a configuration change) and observing both regimes on the same pair set; (b) accumulating much more data so more pairs cross both regimes naturally; (c) a separate analysis tying the universe-level cointegration property of each pair to regime, independent of the bot's selection. None of these are in B1 v1.1's scope; v1.1 is read-only on existing logs.

## Implications for structural review v1.2 (closed)

**None substantive.** Branch A is decided per §9.5 verdict; this v1.1 result is informational. The aggregate RISK_OFF finding from B1 v1 (N=147 directional-corroborated) stands as recorded. The mechanism question (regime-causal vs pair-selection) remains open at this N, but **the question only matters if a future experiment were considering a regime-gating lever** — and the v1.2 closure deferred all next-direction choice to the operator.

**For any future configuration decision:**
- If considering regime-gated entry (blocking RISK_OFF entries entirely), B1 v1's aggregate elevation (23.1% vs 16.5%) is sufficient evidence of *some* lever value; B1 v1.1 doesn't refine whether the lever is the right one.
- If considering universe restriction (dropping high-broken-rate pairs), the per-pair top-15 above gives a starting list, but n_runs≤3 means none of these are robust characterizations on their own.
- If considering both, the v1.1 finding that pair-selection covaries strongly with regime means the two levers may not be cleanly separable — the bot's existing pair-rotation logic already partially does both.

- **Directional consistency with regime-causal** on the 2 available paired pairs (1 shows striking elevation under RISK_OFF, the other shows no change). Not generalizable from N=2.
- **The aggregate RISK_OFF finding from B1 v1 is entangled** with pair-selection at this data structure; clean mechanism isolation requires either configuration changes or much more data than the exp_beta window provides.
